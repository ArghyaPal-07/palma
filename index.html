palma.

A few moves to make.

Enable your camera in a well-lit space, then keep your hands inside the frame.

Open your palm

Face your palm toward the camera with your fingers apart. Palma follows 21 landmarks on each hand.

Make a little movement

The original glowing rings, fingertip particles, and cyan trails follow your hands together. Use the layer switches to show or hide them.

Make a heart

Touch your index fingertips together at the top and your thumb tips together below, leaving a heart-shaped space between both hands.

Video is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.
